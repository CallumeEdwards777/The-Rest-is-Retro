**Retro Brand Kit**

**1. Colour Palette**

**Tone	Hex	Use**

**Walnut Brown	#5A3E2B	Primary logo colour, text, headers**

**Burnt Orange	#B45F3D	Accent, buttons, highlights**

**Cream	#F4EDE2	Backgrounds, text contrast**

**Charcoal Black	#2F2F2F	Secondary text, outlines**

**Muted Teal	#4C7A7B	Optional accent for freshness**





**Tip: Keep the palette warm and earthy — it evokes craftsmanship and nostalgia.**



**2. Typography**

**Font	Style	Use**

**Playfair Display	Serif	Headlines, logo wordmark**

**Raleway	Sans-serif	Body text, navigation**

**Pacifico	Script	Optional decorative accent (e.g., taglines or labels)**





**Pairing tip: Use Playfair Display for elegance and Raleway for readability — they balance vintage charm with modern clarity.**



**3. Logo Usage Guide**

**Primary Logo: Woodmark “Retro” with tagline Everything Vintage**



**Secondary Logo: Wordmark only (no tagline) for small spaces**



**Monochrome Version: Use solid walnut brown or cream for printing**



**Clear Space: Keep at least the height of the “R” around the logo**



**Backgrounds: Works best on cream, kraft paper, or dark wood textures**



**4. Brand Voice**

**Tone: Warm, nostalgic, confident**



**Keywords: Timeless, crafted, authentic, sustainable**



**Messaging Style: Short sentences, vintage flair, friendly but refined**



**5. Applications**

**Website: Cream background, walnut text, orange CTAs**



**Packaging: Kraft paper with cream print**



**Social Media: Use muted teal and orange for highlights**



**Merch: Embossed or engraved logo for tactile appeal**
